# IBEX 2.0 — Accounting Posting Matrix V1

Status: **Design baseline**

All entries below are base-currency balanced and are generated by application/domain posting services inside the same local database transaction as the source business document.

## Required system accounts
- Cash / Bank / Wallet accounts
- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable
- Sales Revenue
- Sales Returns / Contra Revenue
- Inventory Asset
- Cost of Goods Sold
- Purchase Returns / Inventory adjustment handling
- Expense accounts by category
- FX Gain
- FX Loss
- Inventory Gain
- Inventory Loss
- Opening Balance Clearing (setup/migration only)

## 1. Cash sale
Business event: posted sale, fully paid at posting.

Journal:
- Debit: Cash/Bank/Wallet = total received
- Credit: Sales Revenue = net sale total
- Debit: Cost of Goods Sold = COGS
- Credit: Inventory Asset = COGS

If tax becomes enabled, tax liability lines are inserted between revenue and total without changing the balancing invariant.

## 2. Credit sale
- Debit: Accounts Receivable = sale total
- Credit: Sales Revenue = sale total
- Debit: Cost of Goods Sold = COGS
- Credit: Inventory Asset = COGS

## 3. Partially paid sale
- Debit: Cash/Bank/Wallet = paid amount
- Debit: Accounts Receivable = unpaid amount
- Credit: Sales Revenue = total
- Debit: Cost of Goods Sold = COGS
- Credit: Inventory Asset = COGS

## 4. Customer receipt against receivable
- Debit: Cash/Bank/Wallet
- Credit: Accounts Receivable

If settlement creates realized FX difference:
- FX loss: additional Debit to FX Loss
- FX gain: additional Credit to FX Gain
The receivable is relieved using the preserved base-currency carrying amount allocated to the payment.

## 5. Cash purchase
- Debit: Inventory Asset = capitalized purchase value
- Credit: Cash/Bank/Wallet = paid amount

## 6. Credit purchase
- Debit: Inventory Asset
- Credit: Accounts Payable

## 7. Partially paid purchase
- Debit: Inventory Asset = purchase value
- Credit: Cash/Bank/Wallet = paid amount
- Credit: Accounts Payable = unpaid amount

## 8. Supplier payment
- Debit: Accounts Payable
- Credit: Cash/Bank/Wallet

Realized FX gain/loss rules mirror customer settlement.

## 9. Sales return — cash refund
Using original sale linkage when possible:
- Debit: Sales Returns / Contra Revenue = returned selling amount
- Credit: Cash/Bank/Wallet = refund
- Debit: Inventory Asset = original COGS restored
- Credit: Cost of Goods Sold = original COGS restored

## 10. Sales return — customer credit
- Debit: Sales Returns / Contra Revenue
- Credit: Accounts Receivable
- Debit: Inventory Asset
- Credit: Cost of Goods Sold

## 11. Purchase return — cash recovery
- Debit: Cash/Bank/Wallet
- Credit: Inventory Asset = original receipt value removed

## 12. Purchase return — supplier credit
- Debit: Accounts Payable
- Credit: Inventory Asset

## 13. Expense paid immediately
- Debit: Expense account
- Credit: Cash/Bank/Wallet

## 14. Inventory shortage adjustment
- Debit: Inventory Loss / designated adjustment expense
- Credit: Inventory Asset

## 15. Inventory surplus adjustment
- Debit: Inventory Asset
- Credit: Inventory Gain / designated adjustment income

## 16. Warehouse transfer
No P&L or ownership journal entry in V1 because value remains within the same business. Only stock movements are posted. If future multi-branch accounting requires inter-branch accounting, that is a separate ADR.

## 17. Opening balances
Opening balances are migration/setup operations only and must be explicitly tagged.
Examples:
- Customer opening receivable: Debit A/R, Credit Opening Balance Clearing
- Supplier opening payable: Debit Opening Balance Clearing, Credit A/P
- Opening inventory: Debit Inventory Asset, Credit Opening Balance Clearing
- Opening cash: Debit Cash, Credit Opening Balance Clearing

A final setup validation must ensure the opening balance process is balanced before go-live.

## Posting rules
1. No source document can become `posted` unless its journal entry succeeds.
2. A stock-affecting document cannot post unless its stock movement succeeds.
3. Source document, stock movement, payment allocation, and journal entry commit atomically.
4. A failed invariant rolls back the entire operation.
5. Posted journal entries are immutable.
6. Reversal creates a new journal entry with opposite lines and explicit linkage.
7. Every journal entry stores `source_type` and `source_id`.
8. Every line stores transaction currency, rate, original-currency values when relevant, and base-currency values.
